# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

The student in this story is made up. Do not use a real student's story or name.

## Two ways to run it

- **Normal app** (`npx expo start`): follow "Before you go on" below. Breathing takes the full minute.
- **Demo mode** (`npm run demo`): no storage clearing needed. On the Demo screen tap **Start as a first-time visitor** to begin at Welcome. Breathing is one short round, so at step 5 say the talking points quickly or say them on Finished. At step 7 a **Demo contact** appears: tap its **Call** to show what a verified contact does (it shows a note, it does not dial), then point at the real ones that stay off until verified. To run the story again, go back to the Demo screen (shake the phone and tap Reload in Expo Go) and tap **Start as a first-time visitor**.

## Before you go on

- (Normal app only) Clear Expo Go's storage (Android: Settings → Apps → Expo Go → Storage → Clear storage; iPhone: reinstall Expo Go), then open Steady once so it is loaded.
- Stop at the Welcome screen and leave the phone there.
- Turn on **airplane mode**. Keep it on for the whole demo.
- Screen mirroring on, brightness up, sound off.

## The story

**1. The moment (Welcome).** *"Picture a first-year student. It's late, exams start tomorrow, and they feel alone in a new city. They don't want to make an account or explain themselves. They just want something gentle."*
Show the Welcome screen: *"No account, nothing to sign up for. And it says up front: this is not emergency care."* Tap **Continue**.

**2. Adults only (age question).** *"Steady is for adults, so it asks one question, with no date of birth and nothing saved but a simple 'seen' flag."* Tap **I am 18 or older**.

**3. Home.** *"Three things, nothing else: check in, a calm moment, or a real person. No streaks, no points, no notifications. And on every screen there's a Need help now button."* Tap **Daily check-in**.

**4. Check-in.** *"How are you today? Our student taps Stressed."* Tap **Stressed**. Read the reply out: *"That sounds heavy. Would you like to breathe, or talk to someone?"* Tap **Breathe**.

**5. Calm moment (let it run, about one minute).** While the circle breathes, say:
- *"Everything you're seeing runs on the phone. It's in airplane mode right now."* (Show the airplane icon.)
- *"The mood they tapped was never saved. Close the app and it's gone."*
- *"Every word on these screens sits in one file our counsellor reviewer edits, so the wording is theirs, not ours."*
- *"If they want to stop, Stop is always there."*

**6. Finished.** *"How do you feel now? Both answers look the same on purpose, because 'Still hard' should never feel like the wrong answer."* Tap **Still hard**.

**7. Talk to someone.** *"Steady points to real people, pastors and counsellors from local churches, not a bot."* Point at the disabled Call buttons: *"These are switched off on purpose. Steady never lets anyone tap a number nobody has checked. Our reviewer verifies each one, then it lights up."* Tap **Need help now**.

**8. Need help now.** *"If it's an emergency, this screen is one tap from anywhere. Verified emergency numbers and helplines appear here once checked."* Tap **Home**.

**9. Close.** *"Gentle, private, and pointing to real people. Next: our reviewer approves the wording and verifies the numbers, and we test with students."*

## If something goes wrong

- **The app opens on Home, not Welcome:** start the story at step 3 and say the age question appears on first launch.
- **You need to save time:** at step 5, tap **Stop** after one breath. You land on Home. Go to step 7 with **Talk to someone** from Home and skip Finished.
- **Someone asks for a real call:** numbers stay off until verified, by design. That's the safety rule.
