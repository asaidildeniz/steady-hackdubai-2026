How this is built
  • The faith walk-through keeps every sentence of the team's reading, word for word and in order. Screens with a dashed label in the map are additions for the branches: the reflections after “Which sounds most like you?”, the safety check after the Elijah line, writing down a question, the rest pause, and the shared reach-out and check-out.
  • A path is a spine of 8 or 9 beats. Choices change the next screen or two, then rejoin the spine. That keeps the number of screens the counsellor has to review small.
  • Typed text is for the student, not for the app. It is shown back once (“You wrote…”) and checked against the crisis phrase list in content.json. It is never interpreted, stored or sent.
  • The Bible step is a question, not a given. “Not now” skips it.
  • Breathe, Untangle and Reach Out are handed off to the screens that already exist, not rebuilt. In this prototype they are placeholders.
  • “Need help now” is top right on every screen, as in the app. Typing a crisis phrase also goes there. The numbers stay pending until the reviewer verifies them.
  • The ending never promises a reminder. Steady has no notifications and remembers nothing, so it can only invite the student to come back.
